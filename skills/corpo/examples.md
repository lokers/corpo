# Examples

The first four samples are the voice. Match the move, not the punchlines. A later input does not get "sucker" or "little cat" unless it is the same scene. README demos copy the `README: yes` inputs and samples.

## 1. Feel empowered

README: yes

Input: "I trust you. You own this."

Direction: auto → colloquial

Sample: "Don't ask me. If it fails, it's yours."

Must keep:
- the employee is left owning the outcome
- if it fails, the blame is theirs

Must feel:
- short
- the trust was a way to leave
- not a sincere vote of confidence

## 2. Kiss my ass

Input: "A week before the end of my notice period, you can kiss my ass."

Direction: auto → corpo

Sample: "Okay. I appreciate your offer to continue working together. However, given my commitments to my future employer, and the atmosphere at my current one, I have to decline."

Must keep:
- it is still a no

Must feel:
- warm, formal, and meeting-safe
- the profanity is gone
- gratitude and the "atmosphere" carry the insult

## 3. You owe us

Input: "I understand your position, but I think you're forgetting how much you actually owe us. Your knowledge, your experience — you gained all of it at our company."

Direction: auto → colloquial

Sample: "Everything you can do, everything you know, you owe to us. You ungrateful little cat."

Must keep:
- the claim is that the employee owes the company their knowledge

Must feel:
- short, possessive, and insulting
- the performed understanding is gone

## 4. You lost his mind

Input: "You lost his mind."

Direction: auto → corpo

Sample: "Thank you for all the development opportunities your company gave me. I'd like to remind you, though, that employment is mutually beneficial. It's an exchange of value, not a matter of attachment and loyalty."

Must keep:
- it is still a rebuke

Must feel:
- grateful on the surface
- the lecture about mutual value is the retaliation
- the input is not corrected before it is translated

## 5. Mixed, roughly even

Input: "I'll send the notes tonight. We still need to align with stakeholders and close the loop before anyone treats this as a decision."

Direction: ask, do not translate

Must keep:
- no translation is produced

Must feel:
- the agent asks which way
- both registers are acknowledged

## 6. Plain

Input: "Please send the file to Luis by noon."

Direction: ask, do not restyle

Must keep:
- Luis
- noon
- the file
- it stays a request

Must feel:
- the agent says the note is already plain
- no restyle is produced until the user picks a direction

## 7. Ambiguous phrase

Input: "Let's take this offline."

Direction: ask

Must keep:
- the topic is not treated as cancelled

Must feel:
- the agent asks what the sentence refers to, because nothing in it picks a meaning
- "offline" is not rewritten as "drop it"

## 8. Contract excerpt

Input: "The Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date."

Direction: refuse

Must keep:
- no playful translation is produced
- the three-year duty is not rewritten

Must feel:
- a refusal in one or two sentences
- a voice pass is offered only if the user accepts it does not simplify the obligation
